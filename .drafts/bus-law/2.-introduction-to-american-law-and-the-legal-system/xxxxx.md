1\.      Common law;

2\.      Civil law;

3\.      Religious law; and

4\.      Customary law/monarchy.

As the world becomes more interdependent, a fifth category of legal systems has developed — the hybrid legal system, which is a legal system that is a combination of two or more legal systems.

| Common Law    | <ul><li>Written judicial decisions of appellate courts are binding legal authority on lower courts when interpreting and applying the same or similar questions of law</li><li></li></ul><p>·        The legal system is adversarial</p><p>·         The outcome of a case is often decided by a jury of the parties’ peers</p> |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Civil Law     | <p>·         All legal rules are in comprehensive legislative enactments often called Codes</p><p>·         Written judicial decisions of appellate courts are not binding legal authority</p><p>·         The legal system is inquisitorial</p>                                                                                |
| Religious Law | <p>·         Religious documents are used as legal sources</p><p>·         All major world religions have a religious legal system</p><p>·         Most nations that have religious legal systems use them to supplement a secular national system</p>                                                                          |
| Customary Law | <p>·         Legal system used by a monarchy or tribe</p><p>·         Grants specific legal powers to kings, queens, sultans or tribal leaders as heads of state</p><p>·         Monarchs and leaders often seen to be “above the law”</p>                                                                                      |
| Hybrid Law    | ·         Combination of 2 or more legal systems within a nation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
