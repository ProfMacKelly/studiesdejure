# analogies - courts differ

For example, [in Pennsylvania](#user-content-fn-1)[^1], when one person gives another an engagement ring, the ring is a conditional gift, which must be returned if the parties later do not get married. However, [in Montana](#user-content-fn-2)[^2], engagement rings are not conditional—i.e., the rig is an "absolute" gift—and the recipient may keep the ring regardless of whether the parties get married.&#x20;

Futhermore, [in some states](#user-content-fn-3)[^3], the party who "calls off" is considered "at fault," which dictates who is entitled to keep the ring; but in other states, who terminated the engagement is irrelevant.&#x20;
