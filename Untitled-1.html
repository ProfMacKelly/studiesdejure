Sec. I: Did your conclusion provide a concise, clear, and reasonable conclusory statement about whether the defendant (Bradley's Big Buy) likely has liability for the plaintiff's injuries (comprised of one sentence)?

Sec. II: Did your submission explain the rules for establishing liability for negligence and any legal defenses relevant to the facts, as those rules are discussed in the text? To establish liability for negligence:

  • Duty of care: A defendant must breach a duty of care, which depends on how a reasonable person—a person who is careful, conscientious, even-tempered, and honest—would/should have acted in the same circumstances.
    • In the case of retailers and other businesses, the duty of care to business invitees (i.e., customers invited onto the premises to do business) includes warning invitees of foreseeable risks the business knows or should have known existed.

  • Causation of damages: A plaintiff must establish that a defendant's breach of duty of care was both the cause in fact and the proximate cause of their damages.
    • Causation in fact ("but for" causation)
    • Proximate cause (including foreseeability)

  • Relevant potential defenses
    • Contributory/Comparative Negligence
    • Assumption of the risk
